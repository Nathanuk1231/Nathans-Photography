About

I’m Nathan, a photographer from Warrington, mostly chasing light and small details.

I shoot landscapes, nature and wildlife, with some urban and creative experiments mixed in. It’s all done on a Nikon D3200 and a 50mm f/1.8, so a lot of what I’m doing is learning how far a small kit can go.

I like edits that look like the place did on the day, so nothing gets pushed too far.
