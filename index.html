Behind the camera

A bit about me

Still learning. Still curious.
Always taking the camera out.

I’m Nathan, a photographer building a collection one frame at a time.

I’m drawn to nature, everyday places and the small changes in light that can make an ordinary scene feel different. I’m still developing my style, and that’s part of what makes photography interesting to me.

This site is where I want to keep the work I’m proud of, try new ideas and see how things change over time.
